    Tips

    • Same spot, same time. Open the app, read for a minute, press Begin, and set the phone face down.
    • After Begin you get a few seconds to settle. A bell starts the session; three bells end it.
    • Tap the black screen once to pause or end.
    • Don't lock the phone during the sit. The screen stays on but black so the timing keeps working. If it locks or you switch apps, the session pauses where you left it.
    • Sit on the cushion by default. Kneel if your hips are tight. Lie down only when tired, and on Fire day swap the pumping breath for slow belly breaths.
    • If the intention question brings up something heavy, let it be there and return to the breath. You don't need to solve it on the cushion.
